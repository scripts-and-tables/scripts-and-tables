Khazm — Dubai Discount Aggregator

A Dubai directory that shows where your loyalty cards and membership programs actually get you a discount — searchable by venue, area, and deal type.

WebDirectoryDubai

My role: Built and deployed the platform.

Visit live app ↗

Executive summary

Dubai residents and visitors often hold a stack of loyalty and membership cards — Fazaa, Esaad, Entertainer, Zomato, ADCB TouchPoints, Skywards — but rarely know where each one actually earns a discount.

Khazm answers that: a directory of venues across the UAE showing which loyalty programs each one accepts, so people get real value from memberships they already have.

What it offers

  • Program coverageAggregates live offers across major loyalty apps — The Entertainer, Fazaa, ADCB TouchPoints, Zomato, Esaad, Supper Club, Elite Club, and more.
  • Smart filteringNarrow by program, experience type (brunch, spa, staycation), discount type (% off, BOGO), and minimum discount.
  • Venue browsingHundreds of venues organised by area (Dubai Marina, Downtown, Abu Dhabi…), searchable by brand or location.
  • Clear listingsEach venue card shows the business, logo, number of offers, and which programs it accepts.

Tech & status

Khazm runs live as a working platform, helping users in the UAE turn the cards already in their wallet into real savings.
