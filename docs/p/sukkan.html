Executive summary

Residents moving into Dubai's newer master-planned communities — The Pulse, Greenview, Urbana, Expo City, Saadiyat Reserve — often lack a neutral place to follow what's happening around them.

Sukkan is a free, independent community information platform: part reading room, part shared archive, with an optional private workspace where residents can keep their own property notes.

What it offers

  • News deskInfrastructure and community stories written by neighbours.
  • Document libraryPublic master plans, brochures, and RTA maps to download.
  • Photo galleryVisual records of construction progress across communities.
  • Weekly newsletterOptional Sunday recap emails.
  • Private workspaceA personal account to track your own property details, payments, contacts, and issues — visible only to you.

Scope

By design, Sukkan keeps a clear boundary: it doesn't store sensitive documents like title deeds, bank statements, or Ejari agreements, and it isn't a broker, marketplace, or payments provider. It's a community information hub with optional, private record-keeping — focused on Dubai's south-corridor communities.

Tech & status

Sukkan runs live on Railway, giving residents one calm, independent place to stay informed about their neighbourhood and keep their own records in order.
