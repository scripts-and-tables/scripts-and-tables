Subscription ERP

Executive summary

A complete admin console for a business that sells subscription-style digital services: manage customers, the accounts they own (with a primary → secondary billing hierarchy), their orders and card payments, and a separate ledger for vouchers, redemptions and balance transfers.

Built as a fully clickable product — real navigation, search, filters, modals, an auth flow with OTP, a synced email inbox, and a documented sync/API layer — with a light/dark theme and layouts that hold from mobile to desktop.

What's inside

  • Customers & accountsSearchable customer base; accounts arranged as primary workspaces with attached secondaries that inherit billing.
  • Orders & paymentsFilterable orders with line-item detail; a payments view with gross/fees/net and per-transaction breakdowns.
  • Voucher ledgerRedemptions, ping in/out, buy-backs and corrections — with VIP vouchers highlighted across the system.
  • Emails & APIAn inbox synced from an external mailbox, plus a documented API with auth, endpoints, webhooks and rate limits.
